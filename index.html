About The Masterclass

This masterclass offers an immersive journey to elevate leadership skills and drive personal and professional growth. Day 1 focuses on advancing leadership capabilities, addressing key challenges for successful leaders, and learning from exemplary examples. Participants will explore the innovative Feedforward technique to receive feedback constructively and foster future growth. Through interactive coaching exercises and insights from leadership effectiveness research, attendees will gain tools to achieve sustained behavioral improvements and elevate their impact as leaders.

Day 2 shifts focus to stakeholder-centered leadership and personal development. Drawing inspiration from Alan Mulally’s principles, the program emphasizes effective communication, behavioral alignment, and goal-setting. Attendees will also engage in transformative practices like mindfulness, situational leadership, and the MOJO framework to find greater meaning and happiness in their work. The session concludes with the Wheel of Change, guiding participants toward becoming their best selves while embracing valuable support structures for long-term success.

Learn advanced leadership skills and practical strategies to drive personal growth, foster meaningful stakeholder relationships, and build a sustainable, high-impact leadership style from the Leadership Guru - Marshall Goldsmith

Workshop Approach

  • Experiential Learning: Immerse yourself in hands-on activities to gain practical skills and insights.
  • Action Planning: Develop actionable strategies and implementation plans to drive positive change.
  • Interactive Discussions: Engage in dynamic and thought-provoking conversations, exchanging ideas with peers and experts.
  • Collaborative Group Activities: Foster teamwork and collective problem-solving through interactive group exercises, enhancing your collaborative skills.
  • Real-Life Case Studies: Apply theory to real-world scenarios, drawing lessons from actual industry cases.
  • Goal Setting: Define clear and achievable objectives to guide your professional development.

What You Will Learn

  • Enhanced Leadership Skills: Develop strategies to address common challenges and foster continuous personal and professional growth as a leader.
  • Effective Feedback Techniques: Use Feedforward to receive constructive feedback without judgment, fostering a forward-focused growth mindset.
  • Behavioral Improvement Tools: Identify impactful behavioral changes and build accountability mechanisms for lasting improvement.
  • Stakeholder-Centered Leadership: Learn to establish clear leadership standards that align organizational goals and ensure consistent behaviors.
  • Personal and Professional Goal Achievement: Build a structured life plan with daily goals, regular reflections, and a support network to drive meaningful growth.
  • Situational and Transparent Leadership: Adapt leadership styles based on situational needs and promote open learning across organizational levels.
  • Increased Purpose and Satisfaction (MOJO): Apply the MOJO matrix to boost personal happiness, find greater purpose, and understand the impact of triggers on self-control.
  • Sustained Personal Growth Structure: Use the Wheel of Change to create support systems that help achieve meaningful, long-term behavioral and personal growth.
